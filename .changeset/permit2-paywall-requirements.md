---
"@stablecoin.xyz/x402": minor
---

`paywall` (Express and Next.js) now advertises EVM payment requirements in the x402 v2 Permit2 shape the SBC facilitator accepts: CAIP-2 `network`, `amount`, and `extra: { assetTransferMethod: "permit2", name, version }`, without the ERC-2612 `facilitator` spender. 0.4.1 offers could not be paid by Permit2 buyers. EVM `payTo`, `amount` and `asset` are validated when `paywall()` is called, and an unlisted EVM token needs the new `tokenName` / `tokenVersion` options. The handler's payer now comes from the facilitator's verify response. Solana offers are unchanged. A payment is matched to its offer by network, asset, `payTo` and amount, so several offers on one network work; an EVM payment whose echoed offer differs from the route's is refused locally without a facilitator call. `settle: false` (verify-only) now throws on EVM networks: the facilitator's verify does not consume the Permit2 nonce, so one signed payment would be served without limit. A failed settlement that reports a transaction (`settlement_pending`, `duplicate_settlement`) returns it in the 402 body as `transaction` with `errorReason`, so buyers can check it instead of paying again. Next.js: buyer-sent `x-payment-*` headers are dropped before the handler runs, and a handler error after settlement is no longer turned into a 402. The EVM client now reads CAIP-2 offers and never picks a Solana offer, but it still signs ERC-2612, which the facilitator no longer settles; moving it to Permit2 is separate work.

Upgrading from 0.4.1: `paywall()` now throws at startup for `settle: false` on EVM, an EVM `payTo` that is not a non-zero 20-byte hex address, an `amount` that is not a positive integer string, an unlisted EVM `asset` without `tokenName`/`tokenVersion`, and token options on a Solana entry. The 402 `network` is now CAIP-2, while `req.x402.network`, the `x-payment-network` header and `PAYMENT-RESPONSE.network` keep the friendly name. EVM offers no longer carry `facilitator`. The handler's payer now comes from the facilitator.
